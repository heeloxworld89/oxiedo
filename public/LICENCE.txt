DRAFTING NOTE — REMOVE BEFORE RELEASE

This is a working draft prepared for review, not executed legal advice. It follows the
structure of the PolyForm licence family, which is well-tested source-available drafting,
adapted to Oxiedo's terms. Have a solicitor review it before the Software is released,
paying particular attention to: the section 3(b) evidence clause, which is unusual and is
the commercially load-bearing one; the section 4 survival wording, which needs to bind a
successor properly under the law of the governing jurisdiction; the licensor's legal name,
which changes on the Delaware reincorporation; and the governing law and forum clause,
deliberately absent here until that completes.
