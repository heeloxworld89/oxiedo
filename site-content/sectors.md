FIVE MARKETS

## Five industries asking the same question in five different vocabularies.

A bank, a hospital, a training lab, a data controller and a defence programme all need to know what a model did and why. Each calls it something different, whether validation, audit, diagnosis, erasure or assurance, and each is told the same thing by their current stack: that the answer cannot be recovered after training. It cannot. It has to be produced during it.

So these are not five products. They are five readings of one record, under one licence. What differs between them is not the mechanism but the obligation driving it, the person who signs, and how long the sale takes.
